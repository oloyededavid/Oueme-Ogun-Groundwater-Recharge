# Data licence

**Derived data and figures** in `data/processed/`, `outputs/`, `figures/` and `docs/data/` are released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite this repository (`CITATION.cff`).

They are derived from the following sources, which keep their own terms and must also be credited:

- **FAO WaPOR v3** (evapotranspiration, precipitation, reference ET): "FAO WaPOR database", CC BY 4.0.
- **CHIRPS v2.0**, Climate Hazards Center, UC Santa Barbara: Funk et al. (2015), *Scientific Data* 2, 150066. See the CHC terms of use.
- **CHIRPS v3.0**, Climate Hazards Center, UC Santa Barbara (README-CHIRPSv3.0.txt on the CHC data server). See the CHC terms of use.
- **TAMSAT v3.1**, University of Reading: Maidment et al. (2017), *Scientific Data* 4, 170063. See the TAMSAT terms of use.
- **HydroBASINS v1c**: Lehner & Grill (2013), *Hydrological Processes* 27, 2171–2186. HydroSHEDS licence agreement.
- **Natural Earth** country outlines (location map only): public domain.
- **Observed flow at Bétérou**: values published in Proc. IAHS 383, 163–169 (2020), doi:10.5194/piahs-383-163-2020.
