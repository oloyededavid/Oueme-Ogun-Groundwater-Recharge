Methods, data and limits

How this atlas was made

—

1. The question

Groundwater recharge is the rain that soaks down to the water table. Wells are rarely monitored in these catchments, so a common shortcut is to use satellites: rainfall minus evapotranspiration. This atlas tests whether that shortcut works, using the Upper Ouémé, where river flow has been measured at Bétérou.

2. Data

  • Rainfall: CHIRPS v2.0 and v3.0 (Climate Hazards Center, 5 km, 1981–2024) and TAMSAT v3.1 (University of Reading, 4 km). WaPOR v3's own rainfall layer is identical to CHIRPS v3.0.
  • Evapotranspiration: FAO WaPOR v3 actual evapotranspiration and interception, 300 m, 2018–2024.
  • Catchments: HydroBASINS level 9, traced upstream of the Bétérou gauge (Upper Ouémé) and the Oyan confluence (Upper Ogun).
  • River flow: published mean flow at Bétérou, 47.5 m³/s (1971–1987) and 54.5 m³/s (1988–2010).

3. Steps

  • Average each map over the catchment for each year (area-weighted).
  • Rain left after ET = rainfall − evapotranspiration, for each rainfall estimate.
  • Expected river flow = 0.142 × rainfall, the share the river carried in 1988–2010 (the higher of the two measured periods, which gives the smaller gap).
  • Unexplained gap = rain − ET − river flow. Over several years it should be near zero.
  • Storage check: the water-table rise needed to hold the gap underground, with specific yield 0.2–1.5% (Lachassagne et al., 2021).
  • Scale check: published recharge from groundwater levels in Benin basement, about 4% of rainfall (Kotchoni et al., 2019) and 5–24% (Kamagaté et al., 2007).

4. Limits

  • No measured river flow for 2018–2024 was available; the 1988–2010 share is assumed to still apply.
  • No river-flow data were found for the Upper Ogun, so its balance cannot be checked.
  • WaPOR v3 has not been checked against ground measurements in these catchments.
  • Maps are shown at the grid size of their data (5 km for rainfall, 300 m for ET). Nothing finer is implied.
  • 2018–2024 was wetter than 1991–2020, so wet and dry years cannot be compared.
  • Validation against AMMA-CATCH wells is pending data access.
