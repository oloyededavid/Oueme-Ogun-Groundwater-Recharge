## Open: needs data or new analysis, not done here

- No river flow was measured for 2018 to 2024. The residual of 122 to 169 mm per year rests on the 1988 to 2010 runoff ratio (Q = 0.1416 P), a fixed linear transfer. A related Donga study not cited here reports that flow responds more than proportionally to rainfall, which would push the residual in the reported direction.
- The uncertainty in ET, rainfall and the runoff ratio is not propagated jointly. The ranges are scenarios, not intervals, and a combined interval would include zero.
- The validation script has never run on real well data (`data/raw` is absent). Its tests use synthetic hydrographs. It would use the same specific-yield range as the storage argument, it estimates gross recharge while the balance gives net recharge, and it does not exclude pumped wells.
- The specific yield range (0.2 to 1.5%, Lachassagne et al., 2021) was not re-checked against the source.
- CHIRPS v2 and v3 differ by about 109 mm per year for the Upper Ogun (305 mm in 2020), and v2 is the default throughout. The cause was not investigated.
- TAMSAT handling hard-codes the grid size and assumes descending latitude, and its 2018 Ouémé total (1,388 mm) differs from CHIRPS v2 (1,177 mm) without explanation.
- The seven-year rainfall comparison, the area weighting by pixel centre and the lack of any soil-zone storage term are not tested.
